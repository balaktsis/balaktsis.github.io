About Me

I hold a BSc degree in Computer Science with a GPA of 9.58/10 (equivalent to 3.9/4.0), and I am currently a PhD candidate at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh), specializing in Big Data management, Process Science, and Pattern Mining. I work as a Data Engineer and Researcher at Datalab - Data and Web Science Lab, at the School of Informatics, AUTh.

Projects

Aristomate

A mobile app providing a range of features directly to Aristotle University students.

SIESTA

A highly scalable infrastructure designed for performing pattern analysis in large volumes of event logs.

Temporal Violations Detector

An approach to detect temporal violations across event log entries, focused on BPM community cases. It was also integrated in both Query Executor and Incremental Miner components of SIESTA.

Clustering with Spark

An implementation for k-means clustering on a given dataset with high sensitivity in outliers. The approach is utilizing Apache Spark and hierarchical clustering techniques.
